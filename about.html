Our Story

A Small Parlour with a Big Passion for Beauty

Glow started as a small beauty parlour with one chair and a simple dream: to make every woman feel confident and beautiful without spending a fortune.

Over the years, thousands of happy customers have trusted us for their haircuts, facials, festival looks and wedding makeup. Today we are a full beauty studio, but our promise is still the same: personal care, honest advice and a clean, friendly space.

  • Personal attention for every customer
  • Honest advice, no unnecessary add-ons
  • Bridal makeup for every style and budget
